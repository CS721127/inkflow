# Mathematics Example

Inline Pythagorean theorem: $a^2+b^2=c^2$.

The quadratic formula is $x=\frac{-b\pm\sqrt{b^2-4ac}}{2a}$.

A block equation:

$$
\int_0^1 x^2\,dx = \frac{1}{3}
$$

A matrix:

$$
A=\begin{bmatrix}1 & 2 \\ 3 & 4\end{bmatrix}
$$
